Privacy Policy

DA3D Designs Studio (DA3D) · Effective date: September 16, 2026

DA3D Designs Studio is a small business that sells digital printable wall art and print-on-demand posters, mainly through our Etsy shop (DA3DDesignsStudio). We also share our designs on Pinterest. This page explains, in plain English, what information we handle, why, and what we don't do with it.

Who we are

DA3D Designs Studio is operated by Dylan, based in the United States. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email us at da3ddesignswastaken@gmail.com.

Our Pinterest integration

We use a Pinterest developer app to publish pins for our own products and to see how they perform. Here is exactly what that app does:

  • What it accesses: our own Pinterest business account, our own boards and pins, and Pinterest's analytics for those pins (impressions, saves, clicks) - all through Pinterest's official API.
  • What it does: creates pins that link to our Etsy listings, and reads the analytics for those pins so we know which designs people like.
  • What it does not do: it does not read, collect, or store personal information about other Pinterest users. It does not access anyone else's account, boards, messages, or profile. It does not follow, message, or comment on anyone. It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • Where the data lives: the aggregate pin statistics Pinterest reports to us are stored on our own computer to help us decide what to design next. The app's access token is stored on our own computer and is never published.

Pinterest's own handling of your information is governed by the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

Information we collect from visitors and customers

  • This page: collects nothing. It is a static page with no cookies, no analytics scripts, no forms, and no tracking. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log standard server data such as IP address and browser type for security and operations - see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Etsy purchases: when you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy handles your payment and gives us only what we need to fulfil the order - your name, shipping address (for physical posters), and the email Etsy uses for order messages. Digital downloads are delivered by Etsy directly. Etsy's handling of your data is covered by the Etsy Privacy Policy.
  • Print-on-demand posters: physical posters are printed and shipped by Printify and its print partners. We pass them only the name and shipping address needed to deliver your order. See the Printify Privacy Policy.
  • Messages: if you email us or message us on Etsy, we keep the conversation so we can help you and keep a record of the order.

How we use it

  • To fulfil and support your order.
  • To comply with tax, accounting, and platform (Etsy, Pinterest, Printify) requirements.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which designs people like so we can make better ones.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade anyone's personal information. Ever.
  • We do not send marketing emails or run advertising based on your personal data.
  • We do not use your information for anything unrelated to your order or our own shop's operation.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes (generally up to seven years), then deleted. Pinterest analytics are aggregate numbers about our own pins, not about individual people, and are kept as long as they are useful to the shop.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (unless we are legally required to keep an order record). Email da3ddesignswastaken@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also manage the data Etsy or Pinterest hold about you directly through those platforms.

Children

Our products and this site are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
